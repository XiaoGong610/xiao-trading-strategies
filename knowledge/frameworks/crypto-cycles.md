# Crypto Market Cycles (Bitcoin 4-Year Cycle)

## The Pattern

Bitcoin follows a ~4-year cycle driven by **halving events** that cut miner block rewards in half. This reduces new BTC supply, historically triggering a supply shock → price appreciation → euphoria → crash sequence.

| Phase | Timing (relative to halving) | What Happens |
|-------|------------------------------|-------------|
| **Accumulation** | 12-18 months before halving | Smart money buys quietly, prices base after prior crash |
| **Halving catalyst** | The event itself | New BTC issuance cuts 50%. Supply shock begins |
| **Bull run** | 6-18 months after halving | Parabolic price discovery, retail FOMO, media frenzy |
| **Blow-off top → crash** | 18-24 months after halving | Euphoria peaks, 70-85% drawdown follows |
| **Crypto winter** | 24-36 months after halving | Capitulation, projects die, builders build, cycle resets |

**Key observation:** Every cycle has peaked 13-18 months post-halving. The current cycle peaked at exactly 18 months (Oct 2025) — textbook timing.

- **ATH:** $126,000 (October 6, 2025)

### Bull Case: Mid-Cycle Correction, Not the End

The cycle is **maturing and elongating**, not repeating the 77-85% crashes:

1. **Spot ETFs** — IBIT, FBTC, etc. didn't exist before. They create persistent institutional demand with regular inflows. This structural buyer didn't exist in 2017 or 2021.
2. **Corporate treasuries** — Strategy (MSTR) holds 713K BTC, others accumulating. Creates a floor of demand.
3. **Stablecoin infrastructure** — USDC at $78B, USDT at ~$140B. Real economic utility beyond speculation.
4. **CLARITY Act** — first real US regulatory framework advancing (60% odds of passing). Legitimizes the industry for institutional capital.
5. **Halving supply reduction compounding** — each halving reduces new supply further. The 4th halving cut rewards to 3.125 BTC/block. Annual new supply is now <1% of total — approaching gold's scarcity.

**If bull case:** BTC finds support at $60-68K, builds a base, then rallies to $120-150K+ in late 2026/2027 as rate cuts arrive and CLARITY Act passes. Second leg higher.

### Bear Case: Cycle Already Topped

The cycle **peaked on schedule** and the crash hasn't fully played out:

1. **18-month peak timing is exact match** to prior cycles (2013, 2017, 2021)
2. **-44% is milder than prior crashes** (-77% to -85%), but BTC could still have another 30-50% to fall
3. **ETF outflows** — $2.97B in 10 consecutive sessions shows institutional demand is not unconditional
4. **Macro headwinds** — Iran conflict, sticky inflation, "higher for longer" rates all hurt risk assets
5. **Prior cycles bottomed 12-14 months after peak** — that would be Oct-Dec 2026 for this cycle

**If bear case:** BTC declines to $35-50K by late 2026, bottoms in Q4 2026/Q1 2027, then the 5th cycle accumulation begins ahead of the 2028 halving.

### Most Likely Path

**Dampened cycle with a higher floor.** The structural changes (ETFs, corporate treasuries, stablecoins, regulation) suggest:

- Drawdown peak-to-trough: -50% to -60% (not -77 to -85%)
- Implied cycle bottom: $50-63K (vs. $30-40K if old patterns repeat exactly)
- Timeline: bottom in Q3-Q4 2026, recovery begins in 2027

### For Entry Timing

- **RSI <25 on crypto stocks** = contrarian buy signal regardless of cycle position (currently: COIN at 24, MSTR at 11)
- **DCA is the right strategy** — nobody can call the exact bottom in a crypto cycle. Average in over 8-12 weeks.
- **Position sizing matters more than timing** — keep crypto exposure to 5-10% of portfolio max given the volatility profile

### For Strategy Selection

| Cycle Phase | Best Strategy | Why |
|-------------|--------------|-----|
| **Post-peak crash (NOW)** | DCA + CSP | Average into oversold names, sell premium at elevated IV |

| **Blow-off top** | Take profits, sell CCs aggressively | Don't be greedy — trim into strength |
