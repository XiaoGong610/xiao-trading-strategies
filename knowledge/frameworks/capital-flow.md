# AI Capital Flow Framework

## Core Concept
Money cascades down the AI supply chain over time. The opportunity is where capital flows *next*, not where it's already arrived. "Don't chase hot spots — position ahead of the bottleneck shift."

钱不会消失，只会提前流向更高回报的方向。 (Money doesn't disappear — it flows ahead to higher-return areas.)

**2026-28 north star thesis:** 提供更便宜、更多更高效的Token (Provide cheaper, more, more efficient tokens). Every bottleneck exists because of this goal. Every investment thesis should trace back to it.

## Capital Flow Progression (2023-2028)

```
Phase 1: Core Compute (NVDA, AMD)                    ← ALREADY EXPLODED
    → Phase 2: Memory/HBM (MU, SNDK)                 ← CURRENTLY EXPLODING
        → Phase 3: Optical/Networking (COHR, FN)      ← JUST STARTING
            → Phase 4: Data Centers (DLR, EQIX)       ← SPREADING
                → Phase 5: Power/Energy (VST, CEG)    ← NEXT STOP
                    → Phase 6: Inference scaling (ASIC, CPU scheduling) ← EMERGING
```

## Optical Sub-Layer (Track 3 Detail)

The optical track has its own internal bottleneck progression. Capital is rotating from finished modules → upstream substrates/components:

| Layer | What | Key Names | Status |
|-------|------|-----------|--------|
| Transceivers/Modules | 800G/1.6T optical modules | COHR, LITE, CIEN, FN | Accelerating — orders confirmed |
| CPO / Silicon Photonics | Co-packaged optics, on-chip photonics | COHR, LITE, AAOI | Just starting — early high-growth |
| Substrates (InP, GaAs, SOI) | III-V compound semiconductor wafers | AXTI, SLOIF (Soitec), SIVE (Sivers) | Bottleneck — small-cap, low awareness |
| DSP / Drivers | Optical DSP, PAM4 drivers | MXL, MRVL | Established — riding the upgrade cycle |
| Test Equipment | Wafer-level testing for optical/power semis | AEHR | Early bottleneck play |

**Serenity's filter for optical:** Buy small-cap, low-awareness names at supply chain nodes with customer/capacity validation. Sell names with equity dilution, NAV premiums, or opaque structures.

## Narrative → Verification Filter

Capital rotates in two phases within each track:
1. **Narrative phase** — stock re-rates on the story ("AI needs more bandwidth!"). Valuation expands on TAM projections.
2. **Verification phase** — stock re-rates on proof (orders, capacity ramps, earnings beats). Only names with real revenue survive.

**Key transition signal:** When the market starts punishing narrative-only names (no orders) while rewarding earnings-verified names (backlog, revenue growth) — the track has shifted from Phase 1 to Phase 2. This is where you want to be concentrated.

从AI叙事，切到订单、产能、财报验证的硬件公司。(Shift from AI narrative to companies verified by orders, capacity, and earnings.)

## How to Use This Framework

1. **Identify which track is at the inflection point** — that's where the best risk/reward is
2. **Don't chase Phase 0 names that have already re-rated** — NVDA at 60x is "already exploded"
3. **Look for Phase 2-3 names that haven't fully priced in the demand shift** — earlier = more upside, but more risk
4. **Track positioning changes over time** — what's Phase 3 today could be Phase 1 in 6 months
5. **The downstream you go, the longer the time horizon** — Track 1 is a now trade, Track 5 is a 2-3 year thesis
6. **Use the Narrative → Verification filter** — concentrate in names that have shifted from story to proof

## Key Questions to Ask

- Where is the system bottleneck *right now*?
- Which link in the chain will go from "supporting role" to "lead"? (哪个环节会从"配角"变成"主角"?)
- Which company can truly convert this bottleneck into revenue and profit? (哪家公司能真正把这个瓶颈变成收入和利润?)
- Has the market already priced in the shift? (Check fwd P/E vs. growth rate)

## ASIC Inference Thesis
Foundation models will eventually stabilize (human knowledge frontier develops slowly). The large, stable parts are suitable for hardware-specialized chips (ASIC) for fast inference. Domain models and fine-tuning continue on general GPUs. This is like Bitcoin mining moving from GPU to ASIC miners — GPUs become unsuitable for large-scale inference over time.

Bullish for: MRVL, AVGO (custom silicon designers)
Potentially bearish long-term for: NVDA's inference monopoly
