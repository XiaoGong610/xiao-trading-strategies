# MACD (Moving Average Convergence/Divergence) Guide

## Basics
MACD measures momentum direction and strength using two EMAs. Default settings: 12/26/9. Three components:

- **MACD line** — 12 EMA minus 26 EMA. Positive = short-term momentum above long-term.
- **Signal line** — 9 EMA of MACD. Smoothed trend of momentum.
- **Histogram** — MACD minus signal. Shows acceleration/deceleration of momentum.

MACD is a **lagging indicator** — it confirms trends, it doesn't predict them. Use it for confirmation, not as a primary entry signal.

## Signal Types

| Signal | What It Means | Strength |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| **Bullish crossover** (MACD crosses above signal) | Momentum shifting up | Stronger when it happens below zero |
| **Bearish crossover** (MACD crosses below signal) | Momentum shifting down | Worse when it happens above zero |
| **Zero line cross up** | Trend turning bullish | Stronger than crossovers — confirms trend change |
| **Zero line cross down** | Trend turning bearish | Stronger than crossovers — confirms trend change |
| **Histogram growing** | Momentum accelerating | Even if small, direction matters |
| **Histogram shrinking** | Momentum decelerating | Early warning — price may still be rising but losing steam |

## Divergences — The Most Powerful Signal

- **Bullish divergence:** Price makes a LOWER low, MACD makes a HIGHER low → selling pressure weakening, reversal likely. This is the strongest MACD buy signal.
- **Bearish divergence:** Price makes a HIGHER high, MACD makes a LOWER high → buying pressure weakening, top forming. Early warning to trim.

Divergences work best at extremes after extended trends. They are the most reliable MACD signal — don't ignore them.

## MACD + RSI Combos

| RSI | MACD | Interpretation |
|-----|------|----------------|
| Oversold (< 30) | Bullish divergence | **High-probability reversal** — best entry signal |
| Overbought (> 70) | Bearish divergence | **High-probability top** — trim or hedge |
| Neutral (40-60) | Zero line cross up | Trend confirmation — safe to add |
| Neutral (40-60) | Zero line cross down | Trend weakening — hold off on new entries |

## When to Trust vs Ignore

**Trust MACD:**
- Trending markets (strong directional moves)
- Confirming RSI and price action together
- Divergences at extremes after extended runs

**Ignore MACD:**
- Choppy/range-bound markets (constant false crossovers)
- Around earnings (gap moves distort the indicator)
- Very short timeframes (noise overwhelms signal)

## Common Mistakes

- **Trading every crossover** — most are noise in choppy markets. Crossovers WITH the trend >> against it.
- **Ignoring divergences** — the most reliable signal but easy to miss if you only watch crossovers.
- **Using MACD standalone** — it's a confirmation tool, not a primary entry signal. Pair with RSI, price action, and trend.
- **Forgetting it lags** — by the time MACD confirms, the move has started. That's fine — you trade the confirmation, not the prediction.
