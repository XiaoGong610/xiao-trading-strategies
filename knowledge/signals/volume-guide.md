# Volume Guide

## What Volume Tells You
Volume is the truth detector. Price shows what happened; volume shows whether anyone cared. Price move + volume = conviction. Price move without volume = suspect. Always check volume before trusting any other signal.

## Volume Patterns

| Pattern | Meaning | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| High volume breakout | Real move — institutions participating | Trade with it, set targets at next HVN |
| Low volume breakout | Weak/fake — likely to reverse | Don't chase. Wait for volume confirmation or fade it |
| High volume decline | **Distribution** — smart money selling | Respect the move. Don't buy the dip yet |
| Low volume decline | Healthy pullback, not panic | Good accumulation zone for DCA/CSPs |
| Volume dry-up after selloff | Selling exhausted | Bounce likely — start scaling in |
| Climactic volume spike on red day | **Capitulation** | Often near a bottom — best entries happen here |

## Volume Profile (HVN/LVN)

- **High Volume Nodes (HVN)** — price levels with heavy historical trading. Market "agreed" on fair value here. These act as strong support/resistance. Place limit buys and CSP strikes at HVNs.
- **Low Volume Nodes (LVN)** — thin air pockets. Price moves through these fast with little resistance. Don't set targets or stops in LVNs — you'll get blown through.

**Rule:** Buy at HVNs, skip LVNs. If price breaks below an HVN on high volume, the next HVN below is your target.

## Relative Volume

Compare today's volume to the 20-day average. Absolute volume is meaningless across stocks.

| Relative Volume | Signal | Action |
|----------------|--------|--------|
| > 2x average | Institutional activity — something is happening | Investigate. This matters. |
| 1-2x average | Normal | Read other signals normally |
| < 0.5x average | No conviction | Ignore the price move — nobody's behind it |

**Caveats:** Volume spikes on earnings/news are expected, not a signal. Adjust for OpEx weeks (monthly options expiration inflates volume). Unusual volume with no news = someone knows something.

## Volume + RSI Combos

| RSI | Volume | Signal | Action |
|-----|--------|--------|--------|
| < 30 | Spike (climactic) | **Capitulation** — best setup | Aggressive entry. CSP at support, DCA heavy. |
| < 30 | Low/declining | Drifting lower, no flush yet | Wait for the capitulation volume. Don't catch the knife. |
| > 70 | Declining | Momentum fading, distribution starting | Tighten stops. Don't add. Sell premium. |
| > 70 | Increasing | Strong trend, buyers still piling in | Don't fight it. Trail stops, let it run. |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Ignoring volume entirely** — trading on price and RSI alone misses half the picture.
2. **Treating all volume spikes as bullish** — high volume on a red day is bearish (distribution). Direction matters.
3. **Comparing absolute volume across stocks** — AAPL trades 50M shares/day, a mid-cap trades 2M. Use relative volume.
4. **Not adjusting for OpEx** — monthly options expiration (3rd Friday) inflates volume. Don't read signal into OpEx noise.
5. **Buying low-volume breakouts** — the most common trap. No volume = no institutions = no follow-through.
